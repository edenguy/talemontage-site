Privacy Policy

Last updated October 4, 2026

TaleMontage creates travel films from photos and videos you choose. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, how long we keep it, and how to reach us. It applies to talemontage.com and app.talemontage.com.

What we collect

  • The photos and videos you give us. You can share an album link, upload files from your device, or choose photos and videos in your own Google Photos.
  • Details inside those files. When each photo or video was taken, and where, if the file records it. We use these to put your film in order and to show places and your route on a map.
  • Your choices. The format, music, and other settings you pick, and the photos you choose for your film.
  • How our site is used. For each visit we note the kind of device and browser, its screen size, language and time zone, the site that sent you, and the country, region, and city it comes from. We don't keep your IP address. We keep only a one-way scrambled form of it, made with a secret key, used only to share our servers fairly between people.
  • One cookie. A random id in your browser, kept for a year, so the same browser takes its fair turn in our queue, and to make sure a Google sign-in returns to the browser that started it. It holds nothing about you.
  • What you write to us. If you send a suggestion, we keep its text, the screen you sent it from, and your email address if you give one.

Photos from Google Photos

When you choose "Choose in Google Photos", you sign in with Google and pick photos and videos in Google's own picker. We receive only the photos and videos you pick. We can't see the rest of your library.

  • We ask Google only for permission to see the photos and videos you select (photospicker.mediaitems.readonly).
  • We download your selected photos and videos once, to create your film. We read when each one was taken. Google doesn't send where they were taken, so a film made from photos you pick has no map of your route.
  • We don't ask for lasting access to your Google account. We use the permission only while bringing over the photos and videos you selected, and we don't store the access token.
  • Google keeps a record that you allowed TaleMontage, so you aren't asked again next time. You can remove TaleMontage's access at any time in your Google Account, under Security, Your connections to third-party apps & services.

TaleMontage's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How we use your photos and videos

We use them only to create your film and show it to you. We don't share or sell them. We don't use them to train AI or machine learning models, and we don't use them for advertising. No person at TaleMontage looks at them, except when you ask us for help with your film.

How long we keep them

Your photos, videos, and film are automatically deleted from our servers 24 hours after your film is ready. You can delete them sooner: "Delete my photos now" on your film's page removes them at once. Your original album and your Google Photos library are never changed. We keep a short record of each film, without its photos, videos, or film, for 30 days to help with support.

If you buy a film

  • Your payment goes to Paddle, not to us. Paddle is the Merchant of Record: it takes your card details and handles the payment and any tax. We never see or store your card number. Paddle holds its own record of your purchase under its own privacy policy.
  • What we receive: your email address and the details of the order (what was bought, when, the amount, and whether it was refunded). We use your email address only to send you the link to your film, to tell you about a refund, and to help you if you write to us.
  • How long we keep your film after you buy. Your photos, videos, and film are kept for 7 days after your purchase, so you can download them and make the changes included with it. You can choose, on your film's page, to have them kept for 30 days instead; nothing is kept longer unless you choose it. "Delete my photos now" still removes everything at once.
  • After they are deleted we keep only the record of the order, with no photos, videos, or film, for as long as payment, accounting, and refund rules require. We can't make changes or new versions once your media is deleted, and we don't try to recognise an album you send again.
  • Our mail is sent through a mail service that receives your email address and the message, for that purpose only.

Who else handles your data

Our servers run the film creation themselves. Cloudflare carries the traffic between your browser and our servers. Map tiles and place names come from map services we ask with locations only, never your photos. We don't sell or share your information with anyone else.

Your choices

  • Delete your photos, videos, and film at any time from your film's page.
  • Remove Google Photos access from your Google Account.
  • Clear our cookie in your browser at any time.
  • Ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, at the address below.

Children

TaleMontage is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
