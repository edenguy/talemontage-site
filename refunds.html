Refund Policy

Effective October 5, 2026

You watch your whole film before you pay, so you know what you're buying. This page says when you get your money back.

When we refund you

  • If we fail to give you what you bought, we refund you in full, automatically. You don't need to ask.
  • If your film has a real defect we can't fix, we refund you. Write to us and tell us what's wrong.
  • If the law gives you a right to cancel or to a refund, that right applies. It is set out in Paddle's Buyer Terms and Refund Policy, which Paddle keeps current for each country.

Other requests

Our orders are processed by Paddle, the Merchant of Record. Any other refund request is handled by Paddle under its Refund Policy. A request that is fraud or abuse may be refused, as that policy says.

What a refund does

A refund ends access to that film. Its files are removed from our servers, and no more changes or downloads are possible.

How to ask

  • Use the link in your receipt, or look up your order at paddle.net.
  • Or write to us at support@talemontage.com with the email address you bought with. We'll help.
